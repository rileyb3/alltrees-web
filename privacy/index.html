Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AllTrees ("we", "us") is a community map for tree climbers: you can discover trees, add them to the map, log climbs, review them, and connect with other climbers. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep the app's data collection to what the features need.

Information you provide

  • Account details. When you sign up we store your email address and a username. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic account identifiers those services return. Passwords for email sign-up are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored in readable form by us.
  • Profile content. An optional bio and profile photo.
  • Content you create. Trees you add (including their location and photos), reviews, ratings, logged ascents (climbs), favourites, reported conditions, and friend connections.

Information collected automatically

  • Location. With your permission, we use your device location while the app is in use to show trees near you and to place a tree at your current spot. You can decline or revoke this in your device settings; some features (nearby trees, "add at my location") won't work without it.
  • Photos & camera. With your permission, we access your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a tree, review, or profile.

How we use your information

  • To run the core features — the map, tree pages, ascent logging, search, profiles, friends, and sharing.
  • To identify likely tree species from a photo you submit (see "AI species identification" below).
  • To manage your account, and to operate the optional Treemium subscription.
  • To keep the community safe — reviewing reported trees and conditions.

AI species identification

If you use the species-ID feature, the photo you submit and the approximate location are sent — through our server — to our AI provider (Anthropic) to suggest likely species. This is used only to generate the suggestion; we do not use your photos to train models.

How information is shared

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Mapbox — map rendering; receives location/usage needed to display the map.
  • Anthropic — AI species identification, as described above.
  • RevenueCat & Apple — to process and validate Treemium subscriptions. Payment is handled by Apple; we never receive your card details.
  • Apple / Google — if you choose those sign-in methods.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Public content

Trees you add, reviews, ratings, and your username and profile are visible to other climbers in the app. Please don't include information in public content that you want to keep private.

Your choices & rights

  • Revoke location, camera, or photo permissions anytime in your device settings.
  • Edit or delete content you've created.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing us (below). We'll process verified requests within a reasonable time.

Data retention

We keep your account and content while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal data, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations.

Children

AllTrees is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be reflected here.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@alltrees.app

This policy describes the AllTrees mobile app's data practices in plain language. It is provided in good faith and is not legal advice.
